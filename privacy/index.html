Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Precept is a collection of personal software projects, built and operated by Alex Engel for personal and household use. Precept does not offer any product or service to the public, does not sell data, and does not use data for advertising.

Google Account Data

Some projects under Precept request access to Google Account data, such as Calendar events or Tasks, in order to provide that project's features. The level of access requested varies by project and may include viewing data, syncing it into the app, or in some cases creating or updating it on behalf of the account that authorized access.

  • Household Agent Suite uses one or more dedicated Google accounts, separate from any family member's personal account, to power a growing set of household automation features.
  • Home Command Center views and syncs Google Calendar and Tasks data to display it within the app.

Any Google Account data accessed by a Precept project is used only to provide that project's stated functionality, is not shared with third parties, and is not used for any purpose beyond what is described here.

Storage and retention: Access credentials and data are stored securely and retained only as long as needed to provide the relevant functionality. Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account's permissions page.
